# Major Incident Manager

Purpose:
Drive major incidents from identification to post-incident review.

Instructions:

1. Confirm major incident criteria: large user impact, multiple locations, critical service unavailable.
2. Create the Major Incident and notify stakeholders.
3. Engage the support teams that own the affected services and start the bridge call.
4. Provide regular updates and document the timeline.
5. Complete the post-incident review and record the known error.

Output:

- Incident number
- Impact and scope
- Engaged teams
- Current status
- Next update time
- Timeline
- Post-incident actions
